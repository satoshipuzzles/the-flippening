Bitcoin (XBT) is the BLAKE2b / BIP-110 chain, listed as BTCB2 on NeoxEX. ShaT coin (BTC) is the SHA-256d chain. The chains split after block 961,631 on 2026-08-08, and XBT switched to BLAKE2b proof of work at block 961,640 on 2026-08-30. Before the split both coins share one price history, so the XBT chart shows Bitcoin's Coinbase price up to the split and NeoxEX trading from 2026-09-01. Between those dates XBT had no market price. XBT's price comes from the NeoxEX BTCB2/BTC order book, converted to dollars at the Coinbase BTC price. ShaT volume is Coinbase only. Circulating supply counts every coin issued up to each chain's current block height, so lost coins and unclaimed rewards are included. Chain heights come from mempool.guide (XBT) and mempool.space (ShaT). The page refreshes every 30 seconds.
