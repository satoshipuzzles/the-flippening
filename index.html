XBT is the BLAKE2b / BIP-110 chain (listed as BTCB2 on NeoxEX); BTC is the SHA-256d chain. The chains split after block 961,631 on 2026-08-08. XBT's price comes from the NeoxEX BTCB2/BTC order book and is converted to dollars at the Coinbase BTC price. BTC volume is Coinbase only. Circulating supply is every coin issued up to each chain's current block height, so lost coins and unclaimed rewards are counted. Chain heights come from mempool.guide (XBT) and mempool.space (BTC).
