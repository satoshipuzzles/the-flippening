The ShaT Coin logo

The mark puts the Tether diamond where the T belongs — that is the joke, and it is the point. Three colourways, two lockups, every format. SVG is drawn as real shapes, so it stays sharp at any size and prints cleanly. Free to use, no permission needed.

Stickers

Two die cuts: the icon, and the full wordmark as it appears in the video. SVG and PDF are both vector, both at the finished size with 0.125 in of bleed already added — send whichever your printer prefers. The lettering is drawn as outlines, so nothing depends on a font at their end. With cut line files draw the blade path in magenta in a layer named CutContour; send those if they ask for a die line. The white keyline is design, not bleed.
