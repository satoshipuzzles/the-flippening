ShaT Coin sticker artwork
=========================

SVG and PDF are both vector and both at the finished size with bleed. Send
whichever your printer prefers — most take either and many prefer SVG. The PNGs
are 300 dpi, only for shops that will not accept vector.

The text in the wordmark is drawn as outlines, not live type, so nothing depends
on a font being installed at the printer's end.

SHAPES
  icon      the coin with the Tether diamond, die cut to the shape
  wordmark  "ShaT Coin" in full, die cut around the lettering

SIZES
  icon      1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4 in  and  25, 50, 75, 100 mm   (width = height)
  wordmark  2, 3, 4, 5, 6 in  and  50, 75, 100, 150 mm         (that is the WIDTH;
            height follows the lockup, roughly 29% of the width)
  The number in the filename is the FINISHED size after cutting.

BLEED
  0.125 in (3.18 mm) on every side, already included, so the artboard is 0.25 in
  larger than the finished sticker. The same physical distance at every size.
  The white keyline around the artwork is part of the design, not bleed.

CUT LINE
  Files ending -cutline draw the blade path in magenta in a layer named
  CutContour. Send those if the printer asks for a die line, the plain ones if
  they generate their own.

WHAT TO TELL THE PRINTER
  Finished size: as per the filename
  Bleed: 0.125 in all round, already included
  Cut: die cut to contour (CutContour layer)
  Colours: orange #F7931A, teal #009393, ink #111820, white. Supplied in RGB;
  ask them to convert to CMYK if they need it and expect the orange to lose a
  little punch.
